The boat · still for sale

The boat the others were measured against.

Majestic is a 2009 Jeanneau 54 DS, offered in Anacortes by Seattle Yachts. Jacques Fauroux drew the hull and Vittorio Garroni drew the cat-eye house. Jeanneau built her at Les Herbiers. The hull is solid glass below the waterline and cored above, with a balsa-cored deck and Kevlar in the high-load areas. She has a Flag Blue Awlgrip hull with a gold stripe.

She is still for sale. On 19 October 2026 the Seattle Yachts page and the YachtWorld listing both ask $349,500.

She was the first benchmark in this search. A 2018 refit put a new KKMI rig, a Spectra watermaker, 2018 Raymarine electronics with a second autopilot, and the full cockpit enclosure aboard. She also carries 1,125 W of solar on the bimini and Victron charging. The listing gives no year for those. She came off the main shortlist for one reason, the layout. A Jeanneau DS is a raised saloon. It is not the true deck saloon the brief now puts first. The layout section explains the difference.

She sleeps six in three cabins with three heads. The master is forward with a separate shower stall. Two aft cabins have queen berths, and each has its own head.

What is already paid for

Haine standing rigging, all new at KKMI in 2018. Spectra Newport 400 watermaker, 2018. Eberspächer 16 kW hydronic heat, plus a 40,000 BTU engine heat exchanger. Three 375 W Trina panels over the bimini on two Victron SmartSolar 150/60 controllers. Victron MultiPlus 12/3000/120. Dixie Delstar 285 A alternator. Raymarine eS128, Axiom 9, RD418HD radar, and an EV-400 autopilot, all 2018, with the original Raymarine 6002 pilot kept as a switchable backup. UK carbon in-mast main from 6/2019. UK X-Drive genoa and a Matrix asymmetric with a sock, both 2018. ICOM VHF with AIS transmit. Iridium GO. Full enclosure, 2018. Vetus bow thruster.

What the listing leaves open

The house bank is twelve Lifeline AGMs from about 2018. They are at replacement age. No generator is listed and there is no lithium. The tender, the 20 hp Mercury, the Rocna 55 stainless, and the bridle are all excluded from the sale. Holding is 20 gallons for three heads. The Viking raft needs certification. There is no solent, no staysail stay, and no vane. The deep fin draws 7 ft 6 in. The listing calls the mast deck-stepped, but factory sources treat the 54 DS spar as keel-stepped. Four dehumidifiers and two space heaters are on the inventory, so look hard for condensation.

The honest part

A raised saloon, not a true deck saloon.

The 54 DS raises the saloon over the hull and wraps it in Garroni’s cat-eye windows. Sitting at the dinette, you can see out, which is the reason the model sold. The saloon is still not on the cockpit level. You step down from the cockpit through a companionway, and the galley sits lower again along the side. Photographs 18, 19, and 23 show the levels.

The brief now ranks a true deck saloon first, with the cockpit, saloon, and galley on one level behind glass doors. Silver Pearl is the site’s example of that layout. The Wauquiez PS47 studies set the bar for the walk-through. Measured that way, Majestic is a very well-equipped raised-saloon production cruiser. She is not that layout, and no refit changes it. This is why she is a Maybe and not on the home page.

The windows also bring two costs. Bonded cat-eye glass is the first thing a 54 DS surveyor checks. In Mexico sun the saloon turns into a greenhouse unless it is shaded.

What she still does better

Her systems: all four brief items, a 2018 rig, dual autopilots, and AIS transmit. Three cabins and three heads on 55 feet. She lies in Anacortes, so there is no ocean delivery and no truck across the country.

What she does not change

She is still a moderate-displacement production hull. The comfort ratio is under 30 and the bolted iron keel draws 7 ft 6 in. The 2018 wire did not change the shape of the boat.

Power

  • House bank: twelve Lifeline GPL-27T 100 Ah AGMs, about 1,200 Ah at 12 V, dated about 2018. Load-test them. The file plans like-for-like replacement on the low side and lithium on the high side.

  • Victron MultiPlus 12/3000/120. Dixie Delstar 285 A alternator. No generator listed. The plan is solar plus the big alternator.

Rig and sails

  • UK Sailmakers carbon laminate in-mast main, 6/2019. UK X-Drive 110% genoa, 2018. Matrix 4 asymmetric with a sock, 2018.
  • Harken 70 electric primaries. One Harken 46 electric and one manual Harken 46 on the cabin top.
  • No solent, no staysail stay, no vane. The file carries a redundancy line for one of them.

Helm, deck, ground tackle

  • Twin wheels. HybridTouch 12 in and 9 in displays at the helms, with i70 instruments.
  • EV-400 autopilot (2018) with the original Raymarine 6002 kept as a switchable backup. Two P70 controllers and a wireless remote.
  • Teak cockpit sole and seats, teak caprail and rub rail. The side decks are non-skid in the photographs, not teak.
  • Full cockpit enclosure, 2018. Vetus 7 hp bow thruster. No stern thruster.
  • Ultra Marine stainless anchor (2018) on 300 ft of 3/8 in HT chain. The Rocna 55 stainless is excluded.

Engine and tanks

  • Yanmar 4JH4-HTE, 110 hp, 2008. 1,735 hours, with service every 250 hours per the listing. Get the log before anything else.
  • Three-blade feathering Max-Prop. Dual Racors.
  • Fuel 208 gal, water 246 gal, holding 20 gal on the listing. The class sheet prints 191 gal of fuel.

Navigation, comms, safety

  • Raymarine eS128 12 in with an RD418HD radar, and an Axiom 9 in, all 2018.
  • ICOM M506 VHF with AIS transmit. Iridium GO. Backstay SSB antenna, with the nav station prewired. No Starlink listed.
  • GlobalFix Pro406 EPIRB in a ditch bag. Viking 6-person raft, listed as needing certification. Lifesling, horseshoe buoy, twelve life jackets.

Known items on the model

  • Cat-eye window bonds and stained liners.
  • The keel joint and the keel bolts.
  • The upper rudder support. The class had a recall on this part.
  • Rainwater that comes down the mast into the bilge.
  • In-mast furler jams.

One ask, several mirrors

Still listed, and the ask came down.

On 19 October 2026 the Seattle Yachts page (stock 2848172) and the YachtWorld and BoatTrader listing (10100003) were both live at $349,500. The listing photographs are dated March 2026. The Denison copy says it was last updated on 28 July 2026. The same listing is also carried by WS Yacht Brokers, United Yacht Sales Harborview, TopBoats, Bluewater, and Legacy Marine. Some mirror snippets still print an older $399,000 ask, so the price has been cut from $399,000. The date of that cut is not on any public page we found. Listingsport blocked automated access and was not checked.

Matt’s September 2026 file gives the market around her. Rum Runner, a 2007 54 DS in Seattle, at $298,500. Liberté, a 2007 54 DS in Brunswick, Georgia, at $315,000. European 54 DSs at roughly €190,000 to €295,000. Majestic is the expensive US 54 DS because her 2018 work is documented and she is already in Puget Sound.

How the three columns read

Low. The ask, AGMs like for like, a portable generator, a dinghy, raft recertification, a rig inspection, and the furler serviced. This is the ‘windows and AGMs behave’ case.

Mid. The middle of every line, with partial lithium and partial window and redundancy work.

High. Lithium, a diesel generator, window re-bedding, keel and rudder work, a new raft, and a staysail or vane. This is a different purchase.

On the Anacortes dock

Ask these before a number.

  1. The engine log behind the 1,735 hours and the service every 250 hours. Oil analysis.
  2. The KKMI 2018 rigging invoice and wire spec. Were the chainplates pulled?
  3. The cat-eye windows. Any re-bedding since 2009, any liner stains, and how the flanges feel.
  4. A load test on the twelve Lifeline AGMs, and their date codes.
  5. The mast step. Is it keel-stepped as the factory says, or deck-stepped as the listing says? Is there a rainwater path into the bilge?
  6. Keel bolts and the keel-to-hull joint on the survey haul. Was the upper rudder support recall done?
  7. The in-mast furler. A full furl and unfurl at the dock, and the foil’s condition.
  8. Why four dehumidifiers and two space heaters? Look for condensation and mildew behind the liners.
  9. The price of the inflatable tender that is offered separately, and confirmation of what is excluded.
  10. Raft certification and the EPIRB registration.
  11. Spectra and Eberspächer service records.

How to read the range

The low path is the ask, tax at zero, and the low end of every line. It lands at $407,050, just over the $400,000 cap. That includes a dinghy, AGM replacement, a portable generator, a raft recert, and $8,000 of contingency, and it assumes the windows, keel, and rudder survey clean.

The high path at the same price is $539,050. Lithium, a diesel generator, window re-bedding, keel and rudder work, a new raft, and a redundancy package all go to the top. The survey of the windows and the AGM load test decide where in that range she lands.
